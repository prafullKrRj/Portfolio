About the Project

CodeForcesly is a Codeforces helper app that helps you to keep track of your Codeforces submissions, contests, and problems. It provides a clean and intuitive interface to view your submissions, contests, and problems. It also provides a feature to view the problems of a contest. It also allows you to add friends and compare your progress with theirs.
