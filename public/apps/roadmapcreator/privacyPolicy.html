Privacy Policy

Roadmap Creator values your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our application. Please read this policy carefully to understand how your personal data is handled.

1. Information We Collect

Roadmap Creator does not collect, store, or share any personal data with third-party servers.

Data Stored Locally

  • Roadmaps you create using the app, including topics and selected parameters or filters, are saved locally on your device.
  • Saved roadmaps are available in the local database and as images on your device.

Analytics and Crash Reports

To improve our app’s performance and user experience, we use:

  • Google Analytics: For tracking app usage patterns. This helps us understand how users interact with the app.
  • Crashlytics: To identify and fix technical issues.

These services may collect anonymized data that does not identify you personally. For more information, refer to the Google Analytics Privacy Policy and the Crashlytics Privacy Policy.

2. Data Usage

  • Your locally stored data remains private and accessible only to you.
  • No roadmap data, topics, filters, or related information are transmitted to any backend servers or external services.

3. Security of Data

We take appropriate measures to ensure that your data stored on your device is secure. However, the security of your device and local storage depends on your personal device settings and usage.

4. Permissions

The app may require the following permissions to function effectively:

  • Storage: To save the roadmaps as images on your device.

5. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted here, and the updated date will be reflected below.
