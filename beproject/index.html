Your Personal Social Interpreter

An intelligent assistive technology that transforms confusing social interactions into understandable insights, empowering individuals with social anxiety or ASD to navigate their world with greater confidence.

The Challenge

Navigating a world of unspoken cues is a significant hurdle for many. Non-verbal communication, which makes up over 70% of interaction, can be overwhelming, leading to misunderstanding and anxiety.

🧩

Key Impact Areas

  • Social interaction difficulties
  • Communication barriers
  • Educational and employment limitations

The Solution

CueSight provides a discreet, real-time solution. By pairing smart glasses with a mobile app, it detects emotions and delivers private feedback, turning subjective social experiences into objective, actionable data.

💡

Core Benefits

  • Real-time social cue recognition
  • Private, on-device data processing
  • Personalized learning and insights

System Architecture

A robust combination of minimalist hardware and powerful software, designed for reliability, privacy, and scalability.

Hardware Components

  • 📷
    ESP32-CAM Module: A low-power microcontroller with an integrated camera and Wi-Fi, capturing the user's perspective.
  • 🔋
    Power Management: A compact battery system ensures power efficiency for extended, all-day use.
  • 👓
    Ergonomic Eyewear Frame: A lightweight, durable frame designed for comfortable all-day wear.

Software Architecture

  • 🧠
    MediaPipe Framework: Google's ML framework for real-time facial landmark detection and emotion classification on the mobile device.
  • 📱
    Mobile Application: The cross-platform brain of the system for data processing, user interface, and personalized feedback management.
  • 🔒
    Privacy-First Database: All data is stored locally on the user's device with advanced encryption, ensuring complete user privacy and data security.

Privacy-First by Design

Your privacy is paramount. All video processing happens directly on your phone. No facial data or video is ever stored or sent to the cloud. The system only logs anonymous emotional events, giving you full control over your personal insights.
