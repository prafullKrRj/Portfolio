Project Aura

Smart Glasses for Social Cue Understanding

The Challenge: Navigating a World of Unspoken Cues

For many individuals, especially those with ASD or social anxiety, interpreting non-verbal communication is a daily hurdle. This can lead to misunderstandings and social withdrawal.

70%+
Of all communication is non-verbal.

Aura provides a private, data-driven tool to help navigate these complex social landscapes with more confidence.

The Technology Stack

Hardware (Minimalist & Simple)

  • 📷
    ESP32-CAM: A low-cost microcontroller with a built-in camera and Wi-Fi to capture the user's view.
  • 🔋
    USB Power Bank: A standard, pocket-sized power source. No complex wiring needed.
  • 👓
    Glasses Frame: Any simple, thick-rimmed frame serves as the mounting base.

Software (The Brain)

  • 🧠
    MediaPipe Engine: Google's framework for on-device ML, providing real-time face and emotion detection.
  • ⚡
    Feedback Module: Delivers customizable haptic, audio, or visual cues to the user's phone or smartwatch.
  • 🗃️
    Private Database: All interaction data is logged and stored securely on the user's device, ensuring privacy.

The Ultimate Goal: Empowerment

Aura is more than a detector; it's a tool for learning and building confidence. By turning subjective social cues into objective, private data, it empowers users to better understand their world, one interaction at a time.
